Research

Undergraduate researcher, Cheng Lab, Boston University. 2025 to present.

Nonlinear vibrational imaging

I work with two postdocs on two label-free chemical imaging systems: Vibrational Relaxation Energy Fluorescence (VREF) and Stimulated Raman Photothermal (SRP) microscopy. Both read a molecule's vibrations directly, so samples don't need dyes or stains.

My work covers mid-IR generation by difference frequency generation, pump–probe alignment with picosecond lasers, and detecting the vibrational signal. I've run spectroscopic validation on reference samples and analyzed the data in MATLAB and Python.

  • Difference frequency generation
  • Pump–probe alignment
  • Picosecond lasers
  • Spectroscopy
  • MATLAB and Python analysis
